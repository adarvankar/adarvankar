Frequently Asked

What accounting services do you provide?
We offer complete accounting — bookkeeping, tax preparation, financial statements, GST filing, and business accounting for small to medium businesses. All with proper GST invoices.
How long does computer repair take?
Most repairs are completed within 24–48 hours. Complex hardware issues may take 3–5 days. We provide free diagnosis and an estimated timeline before starting any work.
Do you offer warranty on refurbished computers?
Yes! All our refurbished computers and laptops come with a 6-month warranty covering hardware issues. We also offer a 30-day return policy if you're not satisfied.
How do I get a digital signature (DSC)?
Bring your Aadhaar and PAN card to our office. We provide Class 2 and Class 3 DSC. The process takes 1–2 working days and is fully government-approved.
How can I pay online for services?
Visit the "Pay Online" section on this page. Enter your name, phone, select your service, and pay securely via UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or Net Banking through Razorpay. You'll receive a payment receipt instantly.
How do I share my screen for remote support?
Download AnyDesk or UltraViewer from our Download Center above. Run the software, share the 9-digit ID shown on screen with our technician, and we'll connect remotely to assist you.

  • Payment details processed securely through Razorpay (we do not store card numbers).

How We Use Your Information

  • To contact you regarding your service request or complaint.
  • To send service updates, invoices, and receipts.
  • To improve our services and customer experience.

Your data is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing. Payment data is handled exclusively by Razorpay with industry-standard encryption.

Pricing

 Refund Policy

Repair Services

  • If the same issue recurs within 15 days of repair, we fix it free of charge.
  • Service charges are non-refundable once work has been completed and delivered.
  • If we are unable to fix the issue, you will not be charged for labor.

Refurbished Products
